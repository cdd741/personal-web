An AI bedtime companion that turns your day into a personalized sleep story.

Built for adults who are exhausted but still wide awake at 2AM. The whole
experience lives in a single conversation: an agent system tracks intent and
orchestrates onboarding, daytime check-ins, voice customization, and nighttime
story generation, with UI that adapts to each response.
